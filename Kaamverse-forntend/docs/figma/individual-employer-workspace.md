Design the complete authenticated Individual Employer Workspace for KaamVerse, an AI-Powered Trusted Employment, Freelance & On-Demand Gig Marketplace designed specifically for Nepal.

IMPORTANT

This dashboard is NOT for companies.

It is designed specifically for individual employers such as:

Parents
Homeowners
Families
Students
Pet Owners
Individuals
Event Hosts
Small Home Businesses
Anyone needing to hire trusted workers

Unlike the Company Employer Workspace, Individual Employers cannot post Part-Time Jobs, Full-Time Jobs, Short-Term Jobs or Internships.

They can only:

Post Freelance Projects
Post On-Demand Gigs
Hire from the Services Marketplace
Hire Nearby Workers
Hire Workers from their Safe Circle

This workspace should feel like a combination of Airbnb + Uber + TaskRabbit + Fiverr, while remaining visually consistent with the KaamVerse ecosystem.

Follow the design philosophy:

"Powerful Inside. Simple Outside."

The interface must remain beginner-friendly, modern, trustworthy and easy to understand. Users should never feel overwhelmed.

Use generous white space, rounded cards (18–20px), soft shadows, subtle gradients, premium typography, smooth animations and modern SaaS design inspired by Stripe, Airbnb, Notion, Revolut and Linear.

Final UX Goal

The Individual Employer Workspace should feel like a personal AI hiring assistant, allowing users to quickly find trusted freelancers, instantly hire nearby workers for emergency gigs, schedule future gigs, and book services through a clean, beginner-friendly interface. It must remain visually consistent with the KaamVerse ecosystem while emphasizing AI Intelligence, Trust & Safety, and Flexible Employment. Emergency gigs should behave like Uber or Pathao with live GPS tracking, ETA, OTP/QR verification, and SOS features, while scheduled gigs and freelance hiring should provide chat, optional interviews, and AI-powered worker recommendations. The entire experience should feel modern, secure, intelligent, and effortless, demonstrating a unified employment ecosystem rather than a traditional job portal.
